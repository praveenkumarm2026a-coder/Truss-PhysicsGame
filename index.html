Method of Joints — Theory Guide

1. Truss Assumptions

Members are two-force members connected at frictionless pins. Loads act only at joints.

2. Support Reactions

Pin: resists both Rx & Ry.  Roller: resists only Ry (vertical).

3. Equilibrium at each Joint

Σ Fx = 0 and Σ Fy = 0 must hold simultaneously.

4. Sign Convention

Tension (T): member pulls away from joint (+).
Compression (C): member pushes into joint (−).
Zero-force: enter 0 in the magnitude field.

5. Zero-Force Member Rules

Rule 1: Two non-collinear members at an unloaded joint → both zero.
Rule 2: Three members at unloaded joint, two collinear → third is zero.

6. Colour Key

🟥 Red = Tension  |  🔵 Blue = Compression  |  🟡 Gold = Zero-force  |  ⬜ White = Unsolved
